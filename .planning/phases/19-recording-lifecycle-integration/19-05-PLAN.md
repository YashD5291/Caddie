  <what-built>
    Screen capture is now wired into `RecordingCoordinator`: a fresh `ScreenRecorder` per meeting starts right after audio starts (manual and calendar-prompted alike), finalizes on normal stop, device disconnect, error teardown, and app quit, and any video failure is logged, surfaced in the menu bar, and leaves the audio recording untouched. All of it is unit-tested against a mock; these checks exercise the real engine.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    1. **Real capture (criteria 1 + 4).** With the app running from `open -n`, start a manual recording from the menu bar, leave it ~30 seconds, then stop normally and let transcription finish. Then check: `ls -la ~/Library/Application\ Support/Caddie/audio/ | tail -5` shows a `<meetingId>.mov` beside `<meetingId>.m4a`; run `<BUILT_PRODUCTS_DIR>/Caddie.app/Contents/MacOS/Caddie --validate-mov ~/Library/Application\ Support/Caddie/audio/<meetingId>.mov` and confirm it prints `VALIDATE isPlayable=true duration=<~30>` and exits 0. Record the measured duration vs elapsed wall clock. Confirm the meeting shows a completed transcript in the app.
    2. **Anchor logged (STOR-04 in-memory leg).** In the captured log stream, find the `Video anchor for <meetingId>` line and record the reported offset (18-04 measured ~215 ms of setup latency; anything in the low hundreds of ms is expected, and the anchor itself is exact by construction).
    3. **Second meeting in the same session (Pitfall 1 regression, criterion 1).** WITHOUT quitting the app, immediately record a second ~20 s meeting and stop it. Confirm a second distinct `.mov` exists and `--validate-mov` reports `isPlayable=true` with a plausible duration. This is the check that a single shared engine instance would silently fail.
    4. **TCC-denied degrade (criterion 3).** Quit Caddie. In System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording, turn Caddie OFF. Relaunch with `open -n` and record a ~20 s meeting. Confirm: audio records and the transcript completes normally; the menu bar shows the orange "Screen recording stopped — audio was saved" warning during and/or after the meeting; the log stream contains a `Screen capture failed to start` error line; no growing or open `.mov` is left behind (a zero-length file is acceptable, an ever-growing one is not). Re-enable the grant afterwards.
    5. **Quit during recording (criterion 2 + VID-07).** Re-enable the grant, relaunch, start a recording, wait ~20 s, then quit Caddie from the menu bar while it is still recording. Run `--validate-mov` on the resulting `.mov` and confirm `isPlayable=true`; up to ~10 s of tail loss is expected and acceptable (fragmented `.mov`).

    After the checks, run `defaults delete com.caddie.app screenRecordingEnabled` to restore the opt-in default, and stop the log stream.

    Record every measured value (durations, offsets, file sizes, exit codes) in the `## Manual Gate Results` table in `19-VALIDATION.md`. If any check fails, report exactly which one and what was observed — do not mark the gate passed.
