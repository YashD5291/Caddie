<objective>
Raise the disk guard for video without ever costing an audio recording: a pure two-tier decision (block below the 500 MB audio floor; skip video and record audio only below the preset-derived video threshold), an injectable capacity seam so the guard is testable, one preset constant shared by capture and threshold, and the low-disk message on the existing non-fatal video channel.

Purpose: STOR-03 as reinterpreted by D-01, which must be surfaced to the user at plan approval. The roadmap wording "blocked pre-start" holds for the video leg, while the core value (every meeting captured) holds for audio. The tier decision is made in the coordinator precondition in `executeStartRecording` (Claude's-discretion choice): it is the one place with a single capacity snapshot for both tiers and the factory gate (D-03).
Output: coordinator changes plus `DiskSpaceTierTests` (pure) and `RecordingCoordinatorDiskGuardTests` (wiring).
</objective>
