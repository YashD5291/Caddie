<objective>
Give the coordinator the only correct signal that a `.mov` is final: an additive `onFinalized` callback on the Phase 18 engine, fired exactly once from `WriterSink` after `finishWriting` completes (and on the writer-not-writing early return), exposed through the `ScreenRecording` seam and mirrored by the mock.

Purpose: D-13. Research proved `ScreenRecorder.stop()` returns before `finishWriting` completes on every path and that finalize rewrites the file in place, so D-05/D-07 verification after finalize is impossible without this signal; sleeps or polling are forbidden workarounds. The engine file is hardware-verified (18-04): the diff must be minimal and additive, and the existing Phase 18 tests are re-run here. The kill-9 gate re-run happens in 20-08.
Output: engine + seam change, `WriterSinkFinalizeTests`, extended `MockScreenRecorder`, two seam tests in `ProtocolDITests`.
</objective>
